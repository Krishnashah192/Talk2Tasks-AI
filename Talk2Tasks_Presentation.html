The Problem

The Meeting Dilemma: Security & Privacy Bottlenecks

Why traditional cloud meeting bots fail modern corporate teams

🛑 Cloud Bots Blocked by Security Policies
Enterprise IT and confidential client calls actively ban external bot attendees like Otter, Fireflies, and Read.ai.
📉 Note-Taking Fatigue & Missed Deliverables
Taking notes distracts attendees from active discussions. Over 67% of spoken commitments are never formally recorded.
🌐 Multilingual Communication Breakdown
Global teams naturally code-switch (e.g. Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish). Traditional English-only tools garble or drop sentences.
🔒 Uncontrolled Cloud Audio Leakage
Sensitive proprietary discussions stored on 3rd-party servers pose compliance and confidentiality breaches.
